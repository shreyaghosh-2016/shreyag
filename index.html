ABOUT

Research at the intersection of language, reasoning, and the physical world.

I am an Assistant Professor in Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Bhubaneswar. Before joining IIT Bhubaneswar, I was a postdoctoral scholar at The Pennsylvania State University. I received my PhD from IIT Kharagpur.

My research spans natural language processing, large language models, multi-agent systems, travel and mobility intelligence, geospatial AI, misinformation analysis, graph reasoning, explainability, and responsible AI. I particularly enjoy building datasets, benchmarks, and end-to-end intelligent systems grounded in difficult real-world settings.
